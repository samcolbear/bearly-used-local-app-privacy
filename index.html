Privacy Policy

Bearly Used Local App · Last updated: 1 November 2026

About this policy: Bearly Used Local App is a locally hosted tool used by Bearly Used Trading Post to prepare and manage listings. Some features connect to third-party services. This policy describes the information those features are designed to process.

1. Who operates the app

Bearly Used Local App is operated by Sam Colbear, trading as Bearly Used Trading Post, a UK-based seller of pre-owned goods. For privacy enquiries, contact Sam Colbear at samcolbear1988@gmail.com.

2. Information the app may process

Depending on the features used and the permissions granted, the app may process:

  • Product photographs and information entered by the seller, such as product descriptions, condition notes, accessories and prices.
  • Locally stored listing drafts, SKU references, inventory records and application settings.
  • eBay account and authorisation information needed to connect the seller's own eBay account.
  • eBay inventory, offers, listing details and seller business-policy information needed for listing preparation and management.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the app and its authentication or notification endpoints, such as request timestamps and service logs.

The initial eBay connection is intended for the seller's own account and requests inventory access and read-only access to seller account information. The app is not designed to collect buyers' payment-card details or eBay passwords.

3. How information is used

Information is used to identify products from seller-supplied material, generate listing text, organise the seller's inventory, create or manage eBay inventory and offers when those features are enabled, read relevant seller business policies, maintain the seller-authorised eBay connection, and protect the service against misuse.

Information obtained through eBay APIs is used only for the functions authorised by the seller and permitted by eBay's API terms. It is not sold or used for unrelated advertising.

4. AI processing and OpenAI

When the seller requests AI listing generation, product photographs and related text may be sent to the OpenAI API for image analysis and content generation. This information is processed by OpenAI under the terms and data controls applicable to its API services. The seller should avoid uploading unnecessary personal information in product photographs or notes.

OpenAI privacy information: OpenAI Privacy Policy. API data controls: OpenAI API data controls.

5. Where information is stored

The app is designed to run on the seller's own computer. Product photographs, listing drafts and inventory records are intended to be stored locally in the app's data and uploads folders. The seller is responsible for securing the computer, restricting access to the app folder and making appropriate backups.

Authentication and notification components may use Cloudflare's infrastructure to receive secure web requests. Cloudflare may process technical request data and service logs as part of providing that infrastructure. Cloudflare privacy information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

eBay processes information in accordance with its own privacy notice and the permissions the seller grants: eBay User Privacy Notice.

6. Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only where needed to provide the requested functionality, including with eBay for authorised marketplace operations, OpenAI for requested AI generation, and Cloudflare for hosting the authentication and notification endpoint. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. The app operator does not sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Local photographs, drafts and inventory records are retained until the seller deletes them or removes the application data. eBay authorisation can be revoked through eBay and the app's connection can be disconnected. Tokens and other authentication material should be removed when the connection is no longer required.

If the app receives a valid eBay marketplace account deletion notification, it must validate and handle that notification and delete any relevant eBay user data held by the app as required by eBay's developer requirements and applicable law. Technical records may be retained only where necessary for security, audit or legal obligations and for no longer than required.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information, including access controls and confidential handling of API credentials. No internet-connected service or local computer can be guaranteed completely secure. The seller should not share API keys, OAuth tokens, passwords or application configuration files.

9. UK privacy rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances and applicable UK data-protection law, individuals may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. ICO information is available at ico.org.uk.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features, integrations or data-processing practices change. The current version will be published at this address.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app's handling of information, contact Sam Colbear at samcolbear1988@gmail.com.
